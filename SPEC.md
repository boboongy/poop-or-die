# Game SPEC: "toilet simulator poop or die"

Written 2026-09-19 from the owner's answers. Anything marked **(undecided)** is not settled: do not build it without asking.

## Concept
A "holding it in" game. Bob desperately needs to poop. There is a long queue for the toilet. Complete missions and find ways to beat the system so Bob reaches a stall in time.

## Player and NPCs
- Player controls **Bob**.
- **Jijio** and other characters are NPCs (queue members and mission targets).

## Camera
- Mostly third person.
- Switches automatically to first person for: **pulling down pants** (owner, 2026-09-19). **The tissue grab and the wipe are third person** (DECIDED 2026-09-27, overrides "grabbing props = first person" of 2026-09-19). Other actions **(undecided)**.

## Controls
- **(undecided)** Owner has not chosen. Not blocking: the grey-box uses Godot input actions (move, look, interact) so keyboard+mouse or gamepad can be set or changed later.

## Levels
- All levels take place in the toilet environment (20 stalls, sinks, mirrors, lobby).
- Every level has a **different mission**; a level can have **several missions**, all required (in any order) before a stall opens.
- **5 levels** (owner, 2026-09-20). The timer gets shorter each level; failing a mission never costs time.
- **Level 1 (owner decided):** find tissue, then pass it to the person in the stall who needs it. Replaces the stand-in "tissue box to the first Jijio" mission.
- **Level 2 (owner decided): "Wet floor".** A toilet clogs and overflows (flood on the floor), Bob mops/dries the wet floor, and wet floor makes people slip (slip and slide).
- **Levels 3-5 (Claude's PROPOSAL, not approved):** L3 hide and seek (see below) plus rock paper scissors and talking to NPCs; L4 water gun at the odd one out (REPLACED 2026-09-24, see "Level 4" below); L5 dance/rhythm battle (b-boy and rhythm game are one system) as the finale. Timer suggestion 75 / 70 / 65 / 55 / 45 s, to tune by playtest.
- Owner answers 2026-09-20: level outline approved (levels 3-5 as proposed); sounds: Claude finds FREE sounds with a clear licence (placeholders meanwhile); flood = **growing puddle in Godot** (try it); **failing a mission = retry only, never costs time**.
- **Water gun odd one out (owner):** it runs at Bob and gives a horror-style **jump scare**, then shoots him aggressively with a water gun.
- **Hide and seek (owner decided, 2026-09-20; overrides Claude's tag suggestion).** In that level (level 3 slot) there is a random EMPTY stall (an exception to "every stall occupied" for that level), or Bob can hide in a stall WITH a Jijio: the trick is to **stand on top of the toilet seat so only one set of feet shows under the door** (a seeker cannot see two sets of feet). If the seeker catches Bob: **horror jump scare**. The owner will add more props so there is more than one way to win. Notes for the design: the stall doors start 0.3 m above the floor, so feet under the door are naturally visible; needs an "climb on the toilet" action and a seeker that checks stalls by looking under doors.
- Ideas still on the list: NPC text bubbles and conversation, fart / plop / splash sounds.
- **Mopping controls (owner, 2026-09-20):** hold a button, then RIGHT-CLICK on the wet area to mop it; every click plays a mopping animation.

## Level 3 "Hide and seek" (owner answers 2026-09-22; plan APPROVED the same day; SUPERSEDES the older hide-and-seek bullet above where they differ)
**DECIDED (owner):**
- **Seekers:** the 9 queue Jijios and the 2-6 walkers all seek and SPRINT (it should look scary from the normal third-person camera). The 20 stall occupants stay in their stalls. Timer **75 s**. Hiding phase 12 s, then anyone with a clear line of sight within about 8 m catches Bob. Seekers check every stall by looking under the door; when all are checked they stop and go back, a random stall opens (same reward flow as Levels 1-2), Bob uses it.
- **Exactly one random stall is EMPTY.** Empty stall: E on the toilet, Bob hops up and stands on the seat. Occupied stall: Bob **stands on the sitting Jijio's lap**, after a **short talk where he asks the Jijio to shush** (numbered replies like Level 1; a wrong reply just repeats, no time lost). Without the talk the occupant shouts when a seeker passes (noise).
- **A puzzle the player works out alone:** nothing in any text mentions the seat, the lap or feet. First try (stand on the floor in a closed stall) gets caught because the seeker sees the feet under the door gap; the jump scare shows the Jijio's face at the gap lit from below (the under-light is ONLY on the Jijio doing the jump scare), so the player can work out why. A prompt "E  climb" appears only inside a closed stall and does not say why.
- **Caught = jump scare, then the screen says press R** (E does nothing there). No extra props now.
- **Noise (option 2 + 3):** sprinting, a door banged open and the hop make noise; a seeker within about 6 m goes to check that door first. Plus **holding in a fart**: a pressure meter; holding a key clenches it (slower fill, Bob cannot move); at full the fart is released = loud noise.
**PROPOSAL numbers (Claude's first guesses, tune by playtest, not yet confirmed by the owner):** meter fills in about 10 s, clench = 3x slower, drops to 0.3 after a release, fart heard within about 8 m; clench key **F** (free: W A S D Shift E Q Esc F3 F4 R N are used); a fart release comes from the existing synthesised placeholder; the conversation keeps the countdown running.

Liminal, empty, slightly unsettling public-restroom look. Two reference screenshots: a cold blue-teal subway stairwell (glossy tile, bright fluorescent strips, deep dark distance) and a fully green-washed restroom (bright green fluorescent panels, white tile turned green, glossy floor, dark far end). Target: the second image's green wash with the first image's glossy tile and dark, quiet depth.
- Ceiling fluorescent panels as the main light: strong green, cool, slightly harsh.
- Glossy tile: floor reflections and specular streaks on wall tile.
- Dark falloff toward far ends and corners; not evenly lit.
- Some tubes may hum or flicker slightly (optional, subtle).
- Built in Godot (lights are not in the glb): WorldEnvironment + panel lights + reflections + light fog. Exact values are tuned by eye by the owner in the editor.
